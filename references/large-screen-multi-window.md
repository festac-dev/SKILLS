# Large-Screen and Multi-Window Patterns

Use space for meaningful parallel work: master/detail, comparison, inspector panels, previews, timelines, dense tables, and resizable work areas. Keep reading widths comfortable and avoid oversized empty cards or stretched forms.

Test compact windows, snapped half-screen, quarter-screen where relevant, maximized, ultrawide, portrait monitors, and mixed-DPI multi-monitor setups.

Preserve state when resizing or moving windows. Keep dialogs inside the active work area. Restore focus correctly. Define behavior for reopening, multiple instances, and different display scales. Do not require a specific monitor arrangement.
