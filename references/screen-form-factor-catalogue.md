# Screen and Form-Factor Catalogue

Discovery catalogue, not a mandate to support everything.

## Handheld
Compact, standard and large phones; rugged devices; folded/unfolded foldables; dual-screen phones; external phone displays.

## Tablet and hybrid
Small/large tablets; portrait/landscape; split-screen; resizable panes; detachable keyboards; stylus workflows.

## Personal computing
Small notebooks; laptops; desktops; portrait monitors; high-density displays; 4K/5K/6K/8K; ultrawide/super-ultrawide; multi-monitor and mixed-DPI workspaces; snapped and compact windows.

## Shared and distant-view
Smart TVs; streaming boxes; projectors; signage; wall displays; kiosks; public terminals; POS; touch tables.

## Wearable and spatial
Watches; heads-up displays; head-mounted displays; AR/VR/MR; spatial windows and anchored panels.

## Specialized
E-ink/monochrome; automotive; industrial; medical/laboratory; embedded appliances; consoles and handheld gaming.

## Conditions across categories
Rotation; resizing; browser chrome changes; notches/cutouts; hinges; safe areas; virtual keyboard; zoom/text scaling; high contrast/forced colors; reduced motion; sunlight/glare; distant viewing; touch/keyboard/remote/voice; low power and poor connectivity.

For each relevant target define expected behavior and evidence. Browser viewport emulation alone does not prove specialized-device support.
