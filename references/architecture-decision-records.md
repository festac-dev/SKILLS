# Architecture Decision Records

Use an ADR for decisions that materially affect long-term maintenance, security, cost or portability.

- Migration and rollback:
- Review trigger:

Keep records concise. Record why the decision fits this project, not why a technology is generally popular. Revisit decisions when constraints change.

## Application evidence

Record the observation that would cause this decision to be revisited and link its implementation. Use this architectural form for long-lived boundaries; use the broader technical decision record for tooling or delivery choices rather than maintaining duplicate records.
