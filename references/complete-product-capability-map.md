# Complete Product Capability Map

This map is intentionally broad. It is a prompt for discovery, not a requirement to implement every item.

## Strategy and evidence
Vision, outcomes, market context, user research, jobs-to-be-done, personas where useful, assumptions, competitive alternatives, business model, pricing, portfolio, roadmaps, and product analytics.

## Experience and interface
Information architecture, navigation, onboarding, search, forms, tables, dashboards, editors, boards, calendars, maps, timelines, notifications, settings, help, empty/loading/error/offline states, responsive behavior, accessibility, localization, themes, motion, and platform conventions.

## Domain workflows
Identity, organizations, permissions, projects, tasks, content, files, approvals, comments, collaboration, inventory, orders, payments, subscriptions, disputes, devices, learning, health, financial records, and domain-specific state machines.

## Platform and engineering
Frontend, backend, APIs, databases, caches, queues, search, storage, media, real-time systems, integrations, SDKs, webhooks, event processing, background jobs, deployment, migrations, observability, backups, disaster recovery, and capacity.

## Security, privacy, and trust
Threat modeling, secure defaults, authentication, authorization, secrets, encryption, audit, data minimization, retention, deletion, export, abuse prevention, moderation, incident response, and supply-chain security.

## AI and automation
Model/provider abstraction, prompt and context management, retrieval, tool execution, permissions, evaluation, provenance, approval, audit, cost controls, guardrails, fallback, and rollback.

## Business and operations
Billing, entitlements, support, success, legal review, compliance coordination, vendor management, financial planning, unit economics, analytics, experimentation, and release operations.

## Quality and lifecycle
Testing, performance, reliability, compatibility, documentation, migration, versioning, deprecation, maintenance, observability, support readiness, and end-of-life planning.

For each capability: identify user need, evidence, owner, cost, risk, dependency, acceptance criteria, and decision (include, defer, reject, or investigate).

## Application evidence

Use this map to discover omitted dependencies of an accepted journey. Trace an approved feature through its data, permissions, failure recovery and operating needs; do not turn the map’s breadth into a release checklist.
