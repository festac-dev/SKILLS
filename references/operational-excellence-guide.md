# Operational Excellence Guide

## Operability
Every critical service has an owner, health signals, dashboards, alert routing, runbooks, capacity assumptions, backup policy, and incident path.

## Change safety
Use staged rollout, compatibility checks, migration plans, rollback/forward-fix criteria, and post-release verification.

## Incident learning
Maintain a timeline, customer impact assessment, evidence, contributing conditions, recovery validation, and tracked corrective actions.

## Sustainable operations
Reduce alert noise, automate repetitive safe work, document manual interventions, and review toil and on-call burden.

## Evidence
A service is not operationally ready because a document exists. Exercise the procedures and retain results.

## Application evidence

Verify one critical operation after startup and after recovery, not only a health endpoint. Record artifact identity, dependency state and the observed recovery result; an old runbook pass does not establish current service availability.
