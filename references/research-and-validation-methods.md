# Research and Validation Methods

## Discovery
Stakeholder interviews, contextual inquiry, workflow observation, support-ticket analysis, diary studies, surveys, analytics review, and competitor/alternative analysis.

## Evaluation
Usability testing, concept testing, prototype testing, accessibility research, benchmark tasks, pilot deployments, and longitudinal studies.

## Evidence quality
- State who was included and excluded.
- Separate observed behavior from interpretation.
- Note sample limitations and uncertainty.
- Avoid leading questions and confirmation bias.
- Protect consent, privacy, and participant data.
- Triangulate qualitative and quantitative evidence.

## Decision output
Finding, evidence, confidence, affected users, product implication, recommended action, owner, and revisit condition.

## Application evidence

Write the decision a study will inform before selecting its method. Preserve observations, participant context and dissenting results; a prototype usability result does not by itself validate demand, willingness to pay or production reliability.
