# TV, Kiosk, and Specialized Display Patterns

## TV
Design for remote directional navigation, unmistakable focus, readable distant text, large targets, shallow navigation, safe areas/overscan, and predictable back behavior. Avoid hover-dependent and dense data-entry flows.

## Kiosk and public terminal
Make the primary task obvious. Protect unattended sessions, clear personal data after completion/timeout, support accessible reach and language choice, and define offline recovery, device health, and tamper resistance.

## Signage and presentation
Optimize for distance, aspect ratio, orientation, contrast, update cadence, and stale-content indication. Do not imply interaction on passive displays.

## Wearable
Use glanceable information, short actions, minimal typing, low power, and companion-device handoff for complex tasks.

## Automotive and industrial
Treat distraction and physical safety as primary constraints. Use domain rules, clear alarm priorities, guarded hazardous actions, fail-safe behavior, and real-device validation.

## E-ink and embedded
Consider slow refresh, monochrome, ghosting, limited memory/CPU, power, and intermittent connectivity.
