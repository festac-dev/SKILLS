# Sample and Placeholder Asset Policy

## Rule
Files supplied as samples, demos or placeholders (video, images, text, data) must never be treated as real project facts, real customer data or final production content unless the owner says so.

## Handling
1. Label them as sample in the inventory.
2. Use them only to demonstrate layout, behavior, performance or format. Do not derive product claims, statistics, testimonials, names or brand decisions from them.
3. Do not ship sample assets to production by accident: keep them in a clearly named folder, and add a pre-release check for leftover samples.
4. Replace with real, licensed or owner-provided assets before launch; record license and source.
5. Large media: test streaming, compression, poster frames, captions and loading behavior with the sample, then optimize or substitute the final asset.
6. If a sample contains people or personal data, treat it as sensitive anyway.
7. Report clearly in the final report which assets are samples and which are final.

## Checks
Search for filenames and metadata marked sample, placeholder, demo, lorem, test; verify copyrights and licenses before release.
