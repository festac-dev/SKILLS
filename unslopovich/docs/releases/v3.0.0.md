## 5 million reviews

v2 read 1.2 million reviews. v3 reads **5,008,684 reviews of 7,786 apps** on the App Store and Google Play: 21 categories, the top-free charts of the US, UK, India, Canada, and Australia, Google Play down to rank 200, up to 20 English-language storefronts, the last 24 months.

Of the 2,072,794 reviews rated 3 stars or lower, a fresh random sample of 1,200 was read and labelled one by one:

| | App Store | Google Play |
|---|---|---|
| Something just doesn't work (bugs) | 35% | 35% |
| Support, refunds, scams | 22% | 20% |
| The business behind the app (deliveries, drivers, game balance) | 18% | 21% |
| Price, paywall, subscription | 20% | 18% |
| Login and account | 11% | 11% |
| Ads and popups mid-task | 9% | 12% |
| Hard to find or use | 9% | 7% |
| A redesign moved or removed something | 7% | 5% |
| Slow or laggy | 5% | 6% |
| Crashes | 5% | 4% |
| AI forced into the app | 2% | 1% |
| Broken layouts | 1% | 1% |

About a quarter of complaints are still things a design pass can fix (26% and 24%). Bugs, lag, and crashes together are 41% on both stores. "Purple gradient" came up once in 5 million reviews, from someone asking Copilot to bring theirs back.

## 4 stars, as promised

A second sample of 300 4-star reviews, 150 per store. 87% of them on the App Store and 61% on Google Play still name a problem, but a different one: a missing feature leads on the App Store (30%), and support and login trouble mostly disappear (2% or less). Thanks to daniel933912 for asking.

## New

- **Codex plugin.** `codex plugin marketplace add erkamyaman/unslopovich`, then `codex plugin add unslopovich@unslopovich`.
- **Update or remove** steps for every install path in the README, and a short list of what the skill won't do.
- **Examples.** Three before and afters in different styles, and a real run on [QuizHub](https://github.com/ramkrishnajha5/QuizHub), an open-source quiz app, with the full report and diff.

## Research changes

- The corpus is every app whose collection finished, with no 60/40 store split. Each store was collected to its target, rotating through categories so every category is covered evenly.
- The population now includes 4-star reviews. The headline numbers stay on 3 stars or lower; 4 stars are reported on their own.

Method, code, and limits: [research/README.md](https://github.com/erkamyaman/unslopovich/blob/main/research/README.md). Full tables: [research/results/summary.md](https://github.com/erkamyaman/unslopovich/blob/main/research/results/summary.md).
