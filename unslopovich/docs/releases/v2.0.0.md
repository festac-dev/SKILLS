## What users actually complain about, with real data this time

v1's numbers were keyword matches on each app's newest App Store reviews, which for a big app is about one week of complaints. v2 redoes the study properly: **1,201,466 reviews of 1,162 apps on the App Store and Google Play**, 21 categories, apps from the top, middle, and tail of every chart, up to 20 English-language storefronts, the last 24 months.

Of the 529,434 reviews rated 3 stars or lower, a random sample of 1,200 was read and labelled one by one:

| | App Store | Google Play |
|---|---|---|
| Something just doesn't work (bugs) | 29% | 36% |
| The business behind the app (deliveries, drivers, game balance) | 24% | 20% |
| Support, refunds, scams | 22% | 18% |
| Price, paywall, subscription | 20% | 14% |
| Login and account | 11% | 10% |
| Ads and popups mid-task | 10% | 13% |
| A redesign moved or removed something | 9% | 6% |
| Hard to find or use | 6% | 7% |
| Slow or laggy | 5% | 7% |
| Crashes | 5% | 4% |
| Broken layouts | 2% | 2% |
| AI forced into the app | 2% | 1% |

About a quarter of complaints are things a design pass can fix. Bugs, lag, and crashes together are 35% and 43%. Purple gradients: zero mentions in 1.2 million reviews.

Method, code, and limits: [research/README.md](https://github.com/erkamyaman/unslopovich/blob/main/research/README.md). Full tables: [research/results/summary.md](https://github.com/erkamyaman/unslopovich/blob/main/research/results/summary.md).

## New

- **Six agents.** `npx unslopovich` finds Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, OpenCode, Gemini CLI, and GitHub Copilot and installs for each. `--agent`, `--project`, and `--dir` for the rest. Also a Claude Code plugin marketplace.
- **Web apps and websites.** React, Next.js, Vue, Svelte, Astro, Tailwind, plain HTML and CSS. On a phone it checks you didn't build a website; on the web it checks you didn't break one.
- **`npx unslopovich detect`.** 31 deterministic rules over SwiftUI, UIKit, Compose, Android XML, React Native, Flutter, and web source. No model, no network, no dependencies. `--json` and exit codes for CI.
- **Commands:** `review`, `treat`, `scan`, `chart`, `follow-up`.
- **Scores out of 24** across six dimensions. Performance is never scored from code: every report says *Not profiled* until someone profiles.
- **Treatment that doesn't trade one template for another.** It calls the case heavy, light, or healthy first, agrees a five-line direction with you before editing, and checks the result against the calm generated default (white cards, gray borders, one blue accent, an icon in a tinted square on every row, a dot beside every label).
