# Product Demo Evidence Capture

The production workflow is maintained in [Motion Studio](../motion-studio/SKILL.md). This playbook owns the boundary between real project behavior and what the film may demonstrate.

1. Read instructions, README, relevant docs, entry points, CLI/API definitions, config and tests before filming.
2. Capture a safe representative input-to-result path. Keep stdout, stderr, exit status, fixture identity and resulting artifacts in the dedicated work folder. Do not mutate live review stores merely to get a cleaner screenshot.
3. Verify each illustrated number and state against its exact source version. A rejected input's expected total may differ from the repaired output after recalculation; preserve both correctly. Approval binds to the artifact represented, never merely its filename.
4. When terminal footage is reconstructed from captured output, preserve command text and results without implying a new live run. Never fabricate a successful integration, benchmark, customer count or security guarantee.
5. Crop interface evidence around the demonstrated control, keeping enough context to identify the operation. Exclude credentials, private paths, personal data and irrelevant console output.
6. Transfer framing, rhythm and object continuity from references, without adopting their products or claims. Use [reference analysis](../references/visual-reference-analysis.md) for supplied media.

Keep machine timing/evidence data only when useful to implementation and validation. Do not generate compulsory planning or report documents. Export methods and audio/visual checks are owned by [video rendering](../motion-studio/references/video-remotion.md), [audio](../motion-studio/references/audio-pipeline.md) and [QA](../motion-studio/references/qa-checklist.md).
