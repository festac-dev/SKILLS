# Research to Roadmap Playbook

1. Collect observations from users, support, analytics, and stakeholders.
2. Separate evidence, interpretation, and assumptions.
3. Cluster problems by user outcome rather than requested feature.
4. Estimate impact, confidence, reach, effort, risk, and strategic fit.
5. Identify the smallest experiment or release that can reduce uncertainty.
6. Define success, guardrails, and stop conditions before implementation.
7. Review outcomes and update priorities; do not preserve roadmap items merely because they were planned.

## Application evidence

Link each committed outcome to evidence, an acceptance signal and its largest unresolved assumption. Use the next small experiment to reduce that uncertainty before expanding feature scope.
