# Playbook: Launch Day and First 90 Days

## Before launch
Gates passed; rollback rehearsed; monitoring and alerts verified; support channel ready; communication plan; feature flags set; load headroom confirmed.

## Launch day
Staged rollout where possible; watch dashboards; named responders; log issues; avoid unrelated deploys.

## Week 1
Triage defects, review metrics against success criteria, fix top friction points, review costs.

## Days 30 to 90
Postmortem or post-launch review (`POST_LAUNCH_REVIEW`), restore drill, dependency updates, performance review, backlog re-prioritization from real usage, security and accessibility re-check.
