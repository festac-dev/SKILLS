Animated graphics examples

Dependency-free patterns driven by scroll, pointer movement and automatic playback, without playback buttons or tuning sliders. Palettes and sample values are illustrative; adapt them to the project's own identity. Respect reduced motion and suspend hidden scenes.

  • Orbital rotation: 3D rings, drag with inertia, pointer tilt, idle spin, keyboard control.
  • Flowing network: canvas particles along curved connections, throughput-driven speed, cursor repulsion, draggable nodes.
  • Layered parallax: pointer, scroll and device-tilt depth with stable text plane.
  • Shader field: WebGL2 noise flow that bends around the cursor, with static fallback and context-loss handling.
  • SVG path flow: connectors that recompute as nodes are dragged or moved by keyboard, with flowing dashes and packets.

Test: python3 test_examples.py (needs Playwright and Chromium).
