# Video scene route

Probe video, extract uniform and change frames, build contact sheets and a multi-frame fact sheet,
then solve coordinate frame, layout, camera, lights, proxies, scene table, paths, poses, and
transitions. Validate representative spatial/light/action frames plus first and last frames.
When the delivered camera is interactive or explorable, read and apply
`../scene/interactive_world_contract.md`; reference-frame matching and exploration validity are
separate review gates.
