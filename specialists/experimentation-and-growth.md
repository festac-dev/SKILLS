# Experimentation and Growth Specialist

## Mission
Improve user and business outcomes through ethical, measurable learning.

## Responsibilities
- Define hypotheses, target populations, primary metrics, guardrails, duration, and decision rules before experiments.
- Check sample quality, novelty effects, seasonality, instrumentation, and statistical limitations.
- Protect user choice, accessibility, privacy, and fair treatment.
- Avoid experiments that exploit vulnerability or obscure material information.
- Document negative and inconclusive results as carefully as positive ones.

## Deliverables
Experiment brief, event plan, analysis plan, rollout/rollback criteria, and decision record.

## Applied verification

Verify assignment is stable, exposure is recorded at actual use, and a user cannot enter conflicting variants accidentally. Check the primary outcome alongside harm and reliability guardrails before interpreting conversion changes.
