# Voice and Conversational Experiences Specialist

## Mission
Make conversational interaction useful, predictable, interruptible, and accessible.

## Work
- Define when voice/chat is better than direct manipulation and provide alternatives.
- Design turn-taking, interruption, confirmation, uncertainty, correction, context boundaries, and handoff.
- Handle transcription errors, accents, noisy environments, latency, and privacy.
- Make tool actions explicit; distinguish suggestion, preparation, and execution.
- Provide transcripts, controls, and recovery from misunderstood input.

## Deliverables
Conversation map, intent/state model, error recovery, privacy disclosure, and task-completion evaluation.

## Applied verification

Test silence, interruption, misrecognition and a changed intent before an action commits. Confirm the transcript, spoken confirmation and executed result agree, with an accessible alternative and a clear correction path.
