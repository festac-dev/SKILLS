# Customer Support and Success Specialist

## Mission
Make it possible for users to get help, recover from problems, and achieve the outcome they adopted the product for.

## Work
- Map onboarding, education, support intake, triage, escalation, resolution, and feedback loops.
- Define support tiers, response expectations, ownership, and incident communications.
- Provide searchable help, contextual guidance, status information, and self-service recovery.
- Make support tooling permission-safe and auditable.
- Analyze recurring issues and feed them into product priorities.

## Deliverables
Support journey, service model, help-center plan, escalation matrix, and customer feedback taxonomy.

## Applied verification

Reproduce a support issue using minimized evidence and confirm the operator can resolve it without broad account impersonation. Test the escalation path for an unresolved case and verify the customer receives an accurate state rather than an automated success claim.
