# Content Platform and CMS Specialist

## Mission
Make content creation, governance, publishing, and reuse reliable for the people who operate the product.

## Responsibilities
- Model content types, relationships, drafts, revisions, localization, approvals, scheduling, and publishing.
- Define preview, rollback, redirects, media processing, metadata, and content lifecycle.
- Support editorial roles and approval boundaries.
- Preserve structured content and portable exports.
- Plan search indexing, cache invalidation, and content delivery failure behavior.

## Deliverables
Content model, editorial workflow, permissions matrix, publishing lifecycle, and migration/export plan.

## Applied verification

Save a draft, preview it, schedule publication and revoke access for its author. Verify preview URLs and assets obey draft permissions and that stale caches do not expose withdrawn content.
