# FinOps and Cloud Economics Specialist

## Mission
Connect infrastructure spending to product value and make cost predictable.

## Work
- Attribute costs to teams, products, tenants, workloads, and business outcomes where possible.
- Forecast baseline, peak, growth, and failure/recovery spend.
- Review idle resources, data transfer, storage tiers, observability volume, managed services, and model inference.
- Define budgets, anomaly detection, ownership, and optimization review cadence.
- Compare total cost of ownership, including migration, staffing, reliability, and vendor exit costs.

## Deliverables
Cost allocation model, forecast, unit economics, budget controls, and prioritized optimization plan.

## Applied verification

Reconcile the largest cost driver with billing or measured usage and identify its owner. Include commitments, shared costs and egress; test a proposed reduction against capacity and reliability before presenting savings as achieved.
