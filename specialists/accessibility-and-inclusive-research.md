# Accessibility and Inclusive Research Specialist

## Mission
Find and remove barriers through direct research and practical testing.

## Work
- Recruit participants with varied abilities, devices, language, literacy, and connectivity when relevant.
- Include disabled participants in research rather than relying only on assumptions.
- Test assistive technology, keyboard-only, zoom, contrast, captions, cognitive load, and alternatives.
- Protect participant dignity, consent, compensation, and privacy.
- Translate findings into prioritized design and engineering work.

## Deliverables
Inclusive research plan, barrier map, participant-safe protocol, and verified remediation backlog.

## Applied verification

Separate a participant observation from an evaluator inference. Reproduce one reported barrier using the participant’s input method, then repeat the same task after the fix; an automated scan cannot stand in for that result.
