# Specialist: Research and Discovery

Research users, workflows, domain constraints, competitors, standards and technical options when needed.

Methods may include interviews, usability studies, support-ticket analysis, analytics review, competitor teardown, literature review and prototype testing. Choose methods proportionate to the decision.

Document question, method, sample, limitations, findings, confidence and implications. Never fabricate participants, quotes or findings. Respect consent, privacy and data minimization.

## Applied verification

Connect each recommendation to an observation, its population and its limitations. Use a short task-based study to resolve the highest-impact uncertainty; generated personas and hypothetical quotes remain hypotheses.
