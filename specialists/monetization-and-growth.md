# Specialist: Monetization and Growth

Evaluate pricing, packaging, trials, onboarding, activation, retention and upgrade paths only when they fit the product and business goals.

Use transparent pricing and explain limits before users encounter them. Avoid dark patterns, fake scarcity, obstructive cancellation, hidden fees, misleading countdowns or consent manipulation. Measure meaningful user outcomes rather than engagement for its own sake. Do not invent market validation or conversion benchmarks.

## Applied verification

Walk through price disclosure, purchase, renewal change and cancellation using the same plan assumptions. Verify limits and fees remain consistent across marketing, billing and access controls; measure completed user outcomes rather than clicks alone.
