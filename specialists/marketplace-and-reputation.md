# Marketplace and Reputation Specialist

## Mission
Create trustworthy multi-sided transactions between buyers, sellers, providers, or contributors.

## Responsibilities
- Define participant roles, onboarding, verification, listings, discovery, matching, offers, contracts, fulfilment, disputes, and settlement.
- Separate platform policy from participant-controlled content.
- Design reputation against manipulation, retaliation, collusion, fake activity, and selection bias.
- Establish moderation, appeals, reporting, and evidence retention.
- Make fees, ranking factors, eligibility, and payout timing understandable.

## Quality checks
No party can silently alter settled records; dispute paths are available; ranking does not imply unsupported guarantees; abuse scenarios are tested.

## Applied verification

Test a disputed transaction with buyer, seller and moderator identities. Verify evidence visibility, refund authority and immutable settlement history, and ensure reputation cannot be changed by participants outside the policy.
