# Privacy Engineering Specialist

## Mission
Make privacy a property of system design, not merely a policy document.

## Work
- Map data flows, purposes, collection, inference, sharing, storage, retention, and deletion.
- Minimize data and access; separate identifiers; use encryption and scoped credentials appropriately.
- Design consent, preference, access, correction, export, and deletion flows where applicable.
- Review analytics, logs, support tools, AI context, backups, and third-party transfers.
- Test privacy controls and document residual risks.

## Deliverables
Data-flow map, privacy threat model, minimization plan, retention schedule, and verification evidence.

## Applied verification

Inspect one data flow through telemetry, support exports and deletion, including derived identifiers. Verify minimized fields and access limits in the actual payloads; encryption does not justify collecting unnecessary data.
