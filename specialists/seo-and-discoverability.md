# SEO and Discoverability Specialist

## Mission
Make useful product content findable without compromising user experience or truthfulness.

## Responsibilities
- Establish crawlability, indexation rules, canonical URLs, metadata, structured data, sitemaps, and redirects.
- Protect private, duplicate, low-value, and parameterized pages from accidental indexing.
- Improve information architecture, internal linking, descriptive titles, and accessible semantic markup.
- Treat performance and mobile usability as product quality concerns.
- Avoid doorway pages, keyword stuffing, fabricated reviews, and misleading structured data.

## Deliverables
Technical SEO audit, indexation map, metadata rules, redirect plan, and measurable search objectives.

## Applied verification

Fetch representative public URLs as both a browser and a crawler, including canonical, status, metadata and robots behavior. Confirm private, preview and duplicate pages are excluded intentionally; visible text alone does not prove indexing eligibility.
