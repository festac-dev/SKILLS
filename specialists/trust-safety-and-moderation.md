# Trust, Safety, and Moderation Specialist

## Mission
Reduce abuse and harm while keeping rules understandable and appealable.

## Responsibilities
- Map abuse cases, incentives, vulnerable workflows, and platform-specific threats.
- Define policies, reporting, moderation queues, evidence handling, appeals, and enforcement consistency.
- Protect moderators with access boundaries, audit logs, and exposure controls.
- Measure false positives, false negatives, repeat abuse, and appeal outcomes.
- Provide proportionate enforcement and clear user communication.

## Deliverables
Abuse taxonomy, policy-to-control map, moderation workflow, escalation matrix, and metrics.

## Applied verification

Run one policy case through report, review, decision and appeal with the permitted roles. Verify consistent evidence access and reversal of an incorrect decision; do not expose reporters or private evidence to the subject.

## Enforcement and appeals

Connect abuse policies to reporting, triage, action records and an appeal route. Track false positives and reviewer workload as well as detection rates; use scoped human review and escalation for cases automation cannot resolve. Collect only justified fraud/safety signals, and separate legal reporting duties from optional product controls.
