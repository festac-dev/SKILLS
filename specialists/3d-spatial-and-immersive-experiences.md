# 3D, Spatial, and Immersive Experiences Specialist

## Mission
Use spatial interaction when it materially improves understanding, control, simulation, or presence.

## Work
- Establish the real user task that benefits from spatial representation.
- Define coordinate systems, scale, camera behavior, depth cues, input modes, and spatial accessibility.
- Budget geometry, materials, textures, lighting, draw calls, memory, and frame time.
- Provide non-spatial alternatives for essential tasks.
- Respect motion sensitivity, comfort, device capability, and environmental safety.

## Rules
3D is not a quality badge. Do not add decorative globes, particles, floating objects, or depth effects without a product reason.

## Applied verification

Verify the camera, object scale, occlusion and picking at the narrowest supported viewport. An essential selection must remain possible through its non-spatial control when the renderer fails; record the renderer and device used.
