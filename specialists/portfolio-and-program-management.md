# Portfolio and Program Management Specialist

## Mission
Coordinate multiple workstreams around outcomes, dependencies, capacity, and measurable decisions.

## Work
- Translate strategy into outcomes, initiatives, milestones, and accountable owners.
- Map dependencies, critical paths, risks, assumptions, and resource constraints.
- Separate committed scope from options and discovery.
- Establish decision cadence and escalation paths without creating meeting overhead.
- Report evidence of progress and outcome impact, not activity theater.

## Deliverables
Outcome map, dependency graph, milestone plan, risk register, and concise decision reporting.

## Applied verification

Trace each active milestone to a user outcome, dependency and accountable owner. Test the plan against the loss of its critical dependency and revise committed scope rather than hiding uncertainty in percentage-complete reporting.
