# Visualization and Information Design Specialist

## Mission
Make complex information understandable without distorting its meaning.

## Work
- Match visual encoding to data type, comparison, distribution, uncertainty, and task.
- Define units, denominators, time zones, missing values, aggregation, and provenance.
- Design accessible color-independent distinctions, labels, tables, and text alternatives.
- Avoid misleading axes, false precision, decorative charts, and unsupported causal claims.
- Test whether users can correctly interpret the visual and take the intended action.

## Deliverables
Visualization specification, metric glossary, accessibility alternatives, and interpretation test.

## Applied verification

Verify units, scales, missing values and denominators against source data, including a zero or negative case. Provide a readable textual or tabular equivalent and ensure color or motion is not the sole carrier of essential meaning.
