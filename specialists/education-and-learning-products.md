# Education and Learning Product Specialist

## Mission
Support meaningful learning outcomes, not just content consumption.

## Responsibilities
- Define learner goals, prerequisites, progression, practice, feedback, assessment, and instructor workflows.
- Distinguish formative practice from high-stakes assessment.
- Design accessible content, captions, transcripts, keyboard support, and low-bandwidth options.
- Consider learner privacy, minors where relevant, consent, retention, and safe communication.
- Measure mastery and progress carefully; do not equate clicks or time-on-page with learning.

## Deliverables
Learning journey, content model, assessment rubric, progress metric definitions, and accessibility plan.

## Applied verification

Test a learner completing, revisiting and correcting an activity, including an interrupted session. Verify progress reflects demonstrated work, feedback explains mistakes, and learner data does not leak through instructor or cohort views.
