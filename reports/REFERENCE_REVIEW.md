# Visual Reference Review

Reviewed 2026-11-07 for toolkit improvement. The sources below are design examples, not verified product data, model benchmarks, pricing evidence or reusable asset licenses. No source artwork or code was incorporated into the toolkit or apps.

## Coverage

- Time-sampled frames from the nine individually linked videos below, including opening, intermediate and ending states.
- All 12 slides from the UX Snacks carousel and all 7 slides from the Your UX Designer carousel, in order.
- Twelve recent listing thumbnails from each supplied profile. Four Marcelo videos and three Settigation videos were inspected at multiple times. This is a bounded profile sample, not the entire account.
- Earlier references: both supplied TikTok clips, the X collection reel and six GitHub showcase images. The longer tutorial was sampled across its duration.
- Audio was not reviewed. No claim is made about the examples' mix quality, narration or synchronization.

Downloaded videos, carousel images, thumbnails, extracted frames, page dumps and download metadata were deleted after inspection. Only observations and source links are retained.

## Timing recheck after user feedback

The earlier inspection used sampled frames; it was not continuous video playback. On 2026-11-07, the 44.77-second Webforge6 clip and 12.93-second collection reel were inspected again at half-second timestamps (90 and 26 frames). This supports observations about visible states, cuts and approximate transition intervals; it does not establish frame-perfect easing or audio quality.

The car clip shows the assembled subject at 0–1 seconds, separation and camera change around 1.5–3.5 seconds, and return around 4–5 seconds. Its tutorial visibly demonstrates image-to-video generation, conversion to JPG frames, and scroll-driven frame selection; a second demonstration around 34–44.5 seconds shows forward and reverse progression and the handoff into following content. The important transfer is a detailed focal subject, continuous state changes, explicit scroll mapping and page continuity—not a requirement to depict a car or claim live 3D.

The showcase reel moves from a large overlapping preview composition into paired project views around 3.5 seconds, advances through different compositions around 4.5, 6.5, 7.5 and 9 seconds, then changes to a contrasting closing composition around 10.5 seconds. Product subject, type hierarchy and page shape change together. These observed cuts do not establish how every showcased website animates internally.

The existing mobile-carousel review remains a review of ordered stills. Its transferable criteria are list/detail continuity, selection, contextual action and persistent navigation. Static slides provide no direct evidence of transition timing.
