# Distinctiveness and Craft Gate

- [ ] A clear concept connects visuals, interaction, copy, and structure.
- [ ] There is at least one signature moment tied to the product's purpose.
- [ ] Existing identity and user decisions were preserved.
- [ ] The "cover the logo" and "swap the competitor" tests were applied and noted.
- [ ] Typography, spacing, hierarchy, and alignment are deliberate and consistent.
- [ ] Empty, loading, error, success, offline, and edge states are designed.
- [ ] Copy is specific, human, and truthful; no fabricated proof.
- [ ] Performance, accessibility, and responsiveness hold up on real devices.
- [ ] No effect exists only to look advanced.
- [ ] Remaining weaknesses are listed honestly.
