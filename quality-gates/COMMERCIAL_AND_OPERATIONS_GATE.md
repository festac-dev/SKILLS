# Commercial and Operations Gate

- [ ] Pricing, limits, refunds, cancellation, and entitlements are internally consistent.
- [ ] Support ownership and escalation paths are clear.
- [ ] Critical dependencies and vendor costs are understood.
- [ ] Capacity and cost assumptions have evidence.
- [ ] Backup and restore have been tested.
- [ ] Incident response and customer communication are prepared.
- [ ] Accessibility and localization requirements are addressed.
- [ ] Analytics definitions are documented and privacy-aware.
- [ ] Release, rollback, and change communication are ready.
- [ ] The product can be operated without relying on undocumented knowledge.

## Application evidence

Check that displayed fees, billing events and entitlement behavior agree for the supported plan lifecycle. Keep estimated costs and exercised recovery results distinct from promises about future operations.
