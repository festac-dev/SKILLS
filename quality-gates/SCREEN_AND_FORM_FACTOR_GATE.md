# Screen and Form-Factor Quality Gate

- [ ] Targets are selected from user and product needs.
- [ ] Compact, intermediate, and expanded layouts are defined.
- [ ] Content adapts to available space; no arbitrary device-only assumptions.
- [ ] Essential actions remain available at supported sizes.
- [ ] No unintended clipping, overlap, or inaccessible overflow.
- [ ] Large displays use space deliberately and keep reading widths comfortable.
- [ ] Rotation, resizing, split-screen, and fold/unfold are handled where relevant.
- [ ] Safe areas, cutouts, hinges, and virtual keyboards are considered.
- [ ] Zoom, text scaling, high contrast, and reduced motion are tested.
- [ ] Relevant input methods and assistive technologies are tested.
- [ ] Specialized targets use appropriate interaction models and real-device testing.
- [ ] Known limitations and unsupported targets are documented.

Do not claim universal screen compatibility without defined coverage and test evidence.
