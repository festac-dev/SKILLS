# Deprecation and End-of-Life Plan

## Change

## Impact
Usage evidence, integrations, data export needs, contractual commitments, and migration effort.

## Timeline
Announcement, warning period, migration support, read-only phase, shutdown, and final deletion/retention.

## Controls
- [ ] Notices are clear and appropriately timed.
- [ ] Migration guide and support path exist.
- [ ] Compatibility and telemetry are monitored.
- [ ] Data portability and retention are addressed.
- [ ] Security and access after shutdown are defined.
- [ ] Final state is verified and documented.
