## Interaction rules
- Keyboard-only users can complete every essential task.
- Mouse behavior is explicit and consistent where supported.
- Focus is always visible.
- Escape behavior is predictable and documented.
- Disabled controls explain why they cannot be activated.
- Shortcuts do not conflict with terminal or shell conventions without good reason.
- Key bindings are configurable when user workflows justify it.
- Include a no-mouse test and a mouse-assisted test.
