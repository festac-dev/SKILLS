## Lifecycle questions
- Is each field necessary?
- Is collection disclosed and justified?
- Can users correct, export, or delete it?
- Do derived records, logs, caches, and backups follow a documented policy?
- Are legal holds or operational exceptions defined and access-controlled?
- Are data transfers and subprocessors understood?

## Verification
Record the test or evidence proving each lifecycle behavior. Do not claim deletion from backups until the actual backup lifecycle supports that claim.
