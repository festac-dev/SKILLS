- [ ] Images, examples, and symbols are culturally reviewed.
- [ ] Plurals, gender, and grammatical context are handled by localization-aware tooling.
- [ ] Translated content is reviewed in the real interface.
- [ ] Fallback language and missing-translation behavior are clear.
