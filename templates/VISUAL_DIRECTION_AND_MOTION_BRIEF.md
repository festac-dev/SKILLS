# Visual Direction and Motion Brief

Use for a substantial visual or motion change. Omit fields that do not affect the work; this is a working aid, not a mandatory extra deliverable.

## Composition

- Dominant object and reading order:
- Navigation and destination inventory:
- Type, spacing, color and material roles:
- Differences from sibling products beyond logo/color:
- Contribution of each reference to scene, page composition and interaction:
- Narrow-screen order, density and input behavior:

## Motion sequence

| Beat | Object/action | Trigger and clock | Transition and hold | Product meaning | Static/failure alternative |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Specify only the beats needed. Include the loop seam or final resting state, scroll reversal, offscreen suspension and interaction interruption where applicable. Keep actual task targets stable. Separate illustrative state from real work being completed.

For a scene-driven page, include the heading, scene occupancy, surrounding content and handoff to the next section in each beat. If autoplay and scroll coexist, define ownership and resume behavior. For mobile flows, describe the context retained when returning from detail or confirmation.
