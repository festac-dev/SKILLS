## Manual checks
- [ ] Complete keyboard operation and visible focus.
- [ ] Logical focus order and focus restoration.
- [ ] Semantic headings, landmarks, labels, and names.
- [ ] Screen-reader announcements for dynamic updates.
- [ ] Contrast, zoom, reflow, and text spacing.
- [ ] Reduced motion and non-colour status cues.
- [ ] Errors identified and recovery explained.
- [ ] Captions/transcripts or alternatives for media.
- [ ] Touch target size and gesture alternatives.

## Evidence
Record environment, steps, result, severity, screenshot/video or reproduction details, owner, and retest.
Automated scans supplement but do not replace manual testing.
